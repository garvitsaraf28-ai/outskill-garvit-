👋 Who we are

We're GrowthSchool.
And we built Outskill.

01

One belief

You learn a skill fastest from the person actually doing it. So we partnered with the top 1% of instructors to build cohort-based courses in personal and professional growth — and used AI to make that learning personal at a scale a classroom never could.

02

Then AI moved the goalposts

AI changed what "upskilling" even means. Roles started getting rewritten faster than curriculums could keep up — and the real divide stopped being skilled versus unskilled. It became AI-ready versus not.

03

So we built Outskill

Our AI education arm — practical, implementation-first AI training for professionals, teams and enterprises. Today Outskill is one of the largest AI education companies in the world.

Our mission
Make 100 million people AI-ready by 2028.
