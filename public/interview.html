12
Access

Ask for what you need. It changes nothing about how you are assessed.

Interviews are an artificial format, and they suit some brains far better than others. If a small adjustment would let you show us your actual thinking, that adjustment is not a favour — it is the point.

Questions in advance

If processing on the spot is hard for you, ask and we will send the areas we plan to cover.

Extra time, or breaks

Longer to think, a pause partway through, or the conversation split across two shorter calls.

Camera off

Say the word. Plenty of people think better without watching their own face.

A different time of day

Medication, energy, childcare, a different time zone — no explanation needed.

Captions or a written follow-up

Live captions on the call, or key points in writing afterwards.

Something not on this list

Ask anyway. The list is not the limit — it is just a starting point.

Not sure how to word it?

Copy this. You do not owe anyone a diagnosis, a reason, or an apology.

Hi — looking forward to the conversation. One small thing that would help me: [what you need]. Thanks for making that possible.
Outskill to confirm: who this request should be sent to, and the promise that it never reaches the assessing interviewer as a note on the candidate.
13
Afterwards

What happens once you log off.

You will hear from us either way

A no is still an answer, and silence is not one. If the date we gave you passes, chasing us is completely reasonable.

You can ask for feedback

Ask, and we will tell you what we saw. It is more useful than replaying the conversation in your head at 2am.

A no now is not a no forever

Roles change, teams grow, timing shifts. People we said no to have come back and been right the second time.

Outskill to confirm: the actual number of working days until a decision, and whether written feedback is offered at every stage or only after a final round.
