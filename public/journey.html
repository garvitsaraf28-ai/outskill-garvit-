Who does what. What runs what.

Every box in this deck is colour-coded by who or what performs the step. Nothing else is colour-coded, so colour always means the same thing.

Box types
Learner actionSomething the learner does — registers, attends, decides, pays.
Team-ownedA person does this. The team's name is printed on the box.
AutomatedA system does this with no human in the loop. Usually WebEngage.
Money movesA payment, an invoice, or a refund is issued here.
Dead endThe journey stops — no access, rejection, or a failed payment.

Money out · refunds & disputes

One gate. Two conditions. One escalation.

Every refund is decided by the same eligibility gate. If the customer goes to their bank instead of coming to us, a second path opens — and the bank's answer overrides ours.
