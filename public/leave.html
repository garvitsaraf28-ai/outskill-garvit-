🔁 Comp-off — how it actually works

Work an off day,
earn a day off.

1
You work on an off daySay a Sunday or a holiday — because a launch, a webinar or a batch needed you.
2
A comp-off gets credited — on approvalIt's based on approval, not automatic — your manager approves it, and it lands in your leave balance.
3
Use it within 90 daysCredited comp-offs expire 90 days from the date of credit. The system always spends the one closest to expiry first.
4
Take it as a full or half dayApply like any leave, with a comment. Forgot to log it? You can apply for a past day up to 28 days back — but not after the 26th of the month.
Example

Priya works Sunday, May 4th supporting a cohort launch. Her manager approves a comp-off on Monday. Three weeks later she books Friday, May 23rd off with it — well inside the 90-day window — and turns her weekend into a three-day trip. 🏔️

📝 Applying right — the golden rules

Five rules that cover
95% of questions.

📅

Apply in advance, yourself

Apply your own leave ahead of time (self-service, or your manager can apply on your behalf). You can't log a past day yourself — only your manager or HR can (comp-off is the exception: up to 28 days back).

2️⃣6️⃣

The 26th is the cutoff

After the 26th of a month, past-dated leave for that month can't be applied. Log it before the books close.

💬

Always add a comment

Every leave type requires a short comment while applying — one line on why is all it takes.

📄

Know your proof thresholds

Sick > 3 days → medical proof. Paid > 5 days → document proof. Maternity → proof always.

⚖️

Balance = what's accrued today

You can only apply against leave already accrued on the day you apply — not what you'll earn later. Applying on Mar 1 for Apr 12? Only your Mar 1 balance counts.

🚫

Combos that don't work

Paid + Maternity can't be clubbed. Maternity + Menstrual can't be clubbed. And Sick Leave isn't available during notice period.

🗓 The leave year · April → March

What happens
at year-end.

🌴 Paid Leave

Up to 10 unused days carry forward into the new year. No encashment.

🤒 Sick Leave

Expires — balances reset at year-end.

🔁 Comp-offs

Don't wait for year-end — each one expires 90 days after credit.

⚖️ Negative balances

Went beyond quota? Negative balances are nullified and reset to zero.
