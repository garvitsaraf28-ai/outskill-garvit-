🧭 Purpose & scope

Backed by law.
Covering everyone.

This policy is framed in accordance with the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 and its Rules — to ensure a safe and secure working environment for everyone associated with GrowthSchool.

It applies to all individuals connected with GrowthSchool — your employment type doesn't matter:

👩‍💼 Employees — full-time, part-time, contractual 🎓 Interns, trainees & apprentices 🧑‍🏫 Consultants & advisors 🤝 Vendors, clients & business partners 🚪 Visitors & third parties 🌐 Anyone who enters the workplace or engages with GrowthSchool in connection with work

⚠️ Know what counts

What constitutes
sexual harassment.

Any one or more of these unwelcome acts or behaviours — whether direct or by implication:

1

Physical contact and advances

2

A demand or request for sexual favours

3

Making sexually coloured remarks

4

Showing pornography

5

Any other unwelcome physical, verbal or non-verbal conduct of a sexual nature

If such conduct interferes with work, creates a hostile or intimidating work environment, or affects an individual's employment or career decisions — it amounts to sexual harassment.

🏛 Internal Complaints Committee

Who hears
your complaint.

GrowthSchool has an Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) to redress complaints of sexual harassment — constituted in line with the POSH Act, and gender-balanced.

👩‍⚖️

Presiding Officer

A senior woman employee leads the committee

🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Employee Members

At least two members from within the company

🌍

External Member

One member from an NGO or association committed to the cause of women

Term: 3 years from the date of constitution
Members' names & contact details are shared with all employees and displayed on internal channels
