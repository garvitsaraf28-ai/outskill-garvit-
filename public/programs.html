🚀 MOFU · The Accelerator — our flagship

14 days of learning.
One year of everything.

Three variants, one journey. Generalist Domestic and Generalist International share the identical program — only pricing and timezone differ. Engineering follows the same shape but runs in sprints.

1
Friday — Kickoff callPortal walkthrough: live tools, resources, recordings, community access. Every cohort starts here.
2
Sat–Sun — Pre-learningTwo days with provided pre-learning content, so everyone walks into Day 1 ready.
3
Monday — the 14 days beginDomestic: Mon–Fri 7–11 PM + Sat/Sun 10 AM–7 PM IST. International: Mon–Fri 10 AM–1 PM + Sat/Sun 10 AM–7 PM EST.
Generalist: first 7 days learning → next 7 days practical building ("office hours"). Engineering: 7 sprints — learn one day, build it the next, seven times over.
4
The last 48 hours — BuildathonEvery learner ships their own AI product. Not theory — a real build.
5
Then — one full yearOne live workshop every week (Wednesday, sometimes Thursday — pre-informed) with the latest AI updates: 7–9 PM IST / ~10 AM–12 PM EST. Recordings of the 14 days and every workshop for a full year.
🗣 Breakout rooms of 5–7 learners — every class, every workshop: unmute, share screen, ask mentors anything 🏆 3 certificates: Outskill · NSDC · Kairos

💼 BOFU · Catalyst — the earning program

Learning → Building →
Earning.

₹1,95,000 / $6,000 · 6 months (24 weeks) · weekends. Catalyst turns AI skills into income. Entry rule (memorize this): a learner must have completed the Accelerator or a Fellowship first — it's pitched at the end of those programs and can never be bought directly.

🧱

Phase 1 · Weeks 1–14 · Hard skills

Automation (Make, Zapier, n8n, APIs) · client problem mapping · lead-gen & cold-email engines · voice agents (VAPI, WhatsApp bots) · AI agent building (Lindy, Gumloop) · product building (Bolt, Lovable, Cursor, Replit).

🔀

Phase 2 · Weeks 15–24 · Pick a path

Track A — Product Owner (MVP): validate an idea, build the backend, add AI features, launch on Product Hunt, monetize. Track B — Business Owner (MVC): niche down, land clients, outreach engines, retainers, productized services, paid community.

🏁

The finale · Demo Day

Pitch to the cohort and mentors; leave with a live product or a running agency — a portfolio-worthy, revenue-ready asset. Demo Day & immersion run as offline events, announced ~2 months in advance. 3 certificates (Outskill · NSDC · Kairos).

The whole ecosystem
in six lines.

Free → low → high

Mastermind (free) and Bootcamp (low-ticket) fill the funnel; the Accelerator is the main offer; Fellowship & Catalyst are premium.

Route by one question

"Do you code?" Python → Engineering everything. No → Generalist everything.

Three prices to memorize

₹94,999 / $1,199 / $2,995 for Accelerator · ₹1,95,000 / $6,000 for Fellowship and Catalyst.

4️⃣

The certificate ladder

Mastermind 1 → Bootcamp 2 → Accelerator / Fellowship / Catalyst 3 (Outskill · NSDC · Kairos).

5️⃣

One hard gate

Every path is open — except Catalyst, which always requires Accelerator or Fellowship first.

6️⃣

Sell the year, not the days

The Accelerator isn't 14 days — it's 14 days plus a year of weekly workshops, tools, recordings and a global community.
