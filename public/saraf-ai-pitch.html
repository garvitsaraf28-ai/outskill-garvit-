02 / 04
💡 Proposed Solution ≈ 1 min 15

One guided journey.
An AI on every call.
Free the seniors.

Saraf.AI replaces the human trainer with a complete onboarding platform: 11 self-paced levels of company & product knowledge, an always-on AI prospect for unlimited voice mock calls, an AI coach that scores every call, and a live team view for the manager.

1
Ground the AI in real company data
Exact prices, all payment routes (Razorpay, Pine Labs, Shopse, Fibe/Propel, XP), the real 14-day curriculum, and 9 buyer personas distilled from analysis of 4,904 actual enrollees — stated objections AND hidden motivations.
2
Live voice role-play in the browser
Speech-to-text hears the rep, the LLM answers in persona, gender-matched Indian-English text-to-speech talks back. 4 hint-modes × 6 difficulty rounds, from Peer to Director.
3
Rubric-graded coaching, not vibes
A second LLM pass grades the transcript on a weighted 100-point rubric (discovery, routing, objection handling, close) with hard compliance auto-flags — false guarantees or wrong EMI terms cap the score.
4
Close the loop with analytics
Every call feeds the rep's dashboard (skills radar, score trend, next-best-action) and the manager's live leaderboard — readiness becomes a number, not a feeling.
