## What Outskill is

Outskill is an EdTech company that teaches working professionals how to actually use AI.
It runs free weekend AI Masterminds, a low-ticket AI Bootcamp, and its flagship paid
program, the **AI Generalist Accelerator** (plus an **AI Engineering Accelerator** for
technical people). Deeper 6-month programs (Fellowship and Catalyst) exist beyond the
Accelerators.

## The product ladder

- **Free AI Mastermind** — free live weekend workshop (~12–14 hours). Two flavors:
  - **Generalist Mastermind** — Saturday and Sunday, for non-technical people.
  - **Engineering Mastermind** — Friday 7–11pm and Saturday 10am–6pm, for coders; prior
    Python knowledge is a must.
- **AI Bootcamp** — paid, low-ticket entry program covering practical AI implementation.
- **AI Generalist Accelerator** — the flagship: 14-day, live, no-code, hands-on program for
  business professionals. This is the main offer presented during Masterminds and Bootcamps.
- **AI Engineering Accelerator** — 14-day live program for tech professionals comfortable
  with Python.
- **Fellowship and Catalyst** — 6-month deep programs; joined after the Accelerator or
  Fellowship (Catalyst is never joined directly).

## Which program fits whom (routing rule)

- Comfortable writing Python code → **AI Engineering Accelerator**.
- Non-technical, or wants a no-code path (marketing, HR, finance, sales, ops, product,
  founders, doctors, teachers, students…) → **AI Generalist Accelerator**.
- "I know some scripting, not sure" → talk to the team; they'll help you pick the right
  track based on what you want to build.

## Who attends

Attendees come from every background: software engineers, data/business analysts, HR,
marketing, finance, product managers, sales, founders, students, teachers, doctors,
hospitality, manufacturing, operations, customer support, recruiters, designers,
freelancers, and consultants. In the Generalist Accelerator cohorts, most learners start
from zero technical background, and about 76% have 10+ years of work experience — it is
built for experienced professionals, not just students.

## Mentors and credibility

Mentors are active builders shipping real products — including ex-NVIDIA and Adobe ML
engineers, applied scientists, and AI engineers. Over 300+ professionals have come through
the programs. Outcomes are always framed honestly: no guaranteed jobs or salaries, ever.
