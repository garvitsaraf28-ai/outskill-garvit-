# AI Generalist Accelerator — the flagship program

## What it is

A **14-day live, no-code, hands-on AI program** for business professionals across functions
(marketing, sales, operations, product, finance, HR, and more). Day-0 kick-off plus
graduation. You don't just watch — you build, and the program ends with you shipping a real
AI MVP.

**If you can use Google Sheets, you can complete it.** It is NOT for deep ML researchers or
AI engineers (they belong in the Engineering Accelerator).

## Schedule and format

- Weekdays: 7:30–11:00pm IST (10:00am–1:30pm EST). Weekends: 10am–7pm IST.
- **Recordings for every session** — miss a session, watch the recording.
- Two cohort start slots and **one free deferral** to a later batch if the dates don't suit.

## Day-by-day curriculum

- **Day 1 — Gen-AI fundamentals & your own AI playground:** prompt engineering, deploying
  local models (Ollama, MSTY), APIs and webhooks.
- **Day 2 — Image, video & AI clones:** clone your face, voice and image; create content
  with tools like ElevenLabs and Suno.
- **Day 3 — AI automations with n8n:** replace repetitive tasks; build a lead-management
  system.
- **Day 4 — Agentic AI:** build your first "AI employee".
- **Day 5 — Beyond prompting:** MCPs — Claude, Perplexity, and building your own MCP server.
- **Day 6 — Real-time human-like voice agents + Tech 101.**
- **Day 7 — Build Day:** ideate → design → build → ship an AI MVP in about 6 hours.
- **Days 8–13 — Office hours + build days:** troubleshooting, team formation, building your
  MVP, pitch submissions.
- **Day 14 — Graduation + community networking.**
