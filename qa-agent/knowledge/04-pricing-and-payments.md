# Pricing, EMI and payment options (AI Generalist Accelerator)

## The prices (exact — never invent, discount, or undercut)

| Situation | Price |
|---|---|
| India (attended the Indian mastermind) | **₹94,999** |
| International (attended the international mastermind) | **$2,995** |
| Pays in USD but attended the **Indian** mastermind | **$1,199** |

There are **no scholarships and no discounts**. The AI Engineering Accelerator price is not
publicly listed — the team confirms current pricing on request.

## India — payment options

- **One-time full payment:** Razorpay link. No extra charge.
- **EMI with a credit card:** Pine Labs. Tenures of 3 / 6 / 9 / 12 months.
  **Zero-cost EMI — Outskill bears the interest**; the learner never pays a rupee extra.
- **EMI without a credit card (debit-card EMI):** Shopse. 3 / 6 / 9 / 12 months, zero-cost.
  Requires passing a quick debit-card-EMI eligibility check on your mobile number first.
- **No credit card and not Shopse-eligible:** NBFC partners **Fibe / Propel** via a Google
  form — fill the form, send the confirmation screenshot, and the Fibe/Propel team handles
  the rest. 3 / 6 / 9 / 12 months, zero-cost.

## International — payment options (XP gateway)

- **One-time full payment:** XP. No convenience fee.
- **Installments:** XP over 3 / 6 / 9 / 12 months with a **9% convenience fee** added to the
  total (always disclosed upfront).

## The rules that must never be gotten wrong

- Indian EMI is **zero-cost** — never tell an Indian learner EMI costs extra.
- The 9% fee applies to **international installments only**.
- EMI tenures are always **3, 6, 9, or 12 months** — no other tenure exists.
- Refund: full within 7 days of enrollment; after that, deferral to a later batch.

## Employer funding

Leaders and managers can often route the fee through their company's L&D budget; a corporate
invoice can be arranged — ask the team.
