# Free AI Mastermind and AI Bootcamp

## Free AI Mastermind

A free, live weekend workshop (~12–14 hours total) that introduces AI and its fundamentals,
with live demos of real tools. Two flavors:

- **Generalist Mastermind** — Saturday & Sunday, for non-technical people. No coding needed.
- **Engineering Mastermind** — Friday 7–11pm & Saturday 10am–6pm, for coders. Prior Python
  knowledge is a **must** for the Engineering mastermind.

### What the mastermind covers (so you can reference what participants just saw)

- Why AI changes every week — and why staying current matters (the Accelerator includes ~54
  weekly live update sessions for a year for exactly this reason).
- The income angle: AI consulting and freelancing as "an ocean of opportunity" — automating
  for small businesses, building side income.
- Live tool demos: local models (Ollama / MSTY); image, video & voice cloning (ElevenLabs,
  Suno); n8n automations (positioned as the easiest, safest, most economical option vs
  Zapier / Make / Power Automate / UiPath); MCPs; real-time voice agents; agentic AI.

During the mastermind, the paid programs (Bootcamp and Accelerator) are presented. You can
join the Accelerator (or Fellowship) right after a mastermind.

## AI Bootcamp

A paid, **low-ticket** program that goes deeper than the mastermind into practical AI
implementation — hands-on exposure rather than just concepts. The Bootcamp also introduces
the Accelerator for those who want the full 14-day guided build experience.

### Bootcamp vs Accelerator — how to think about it

- **Bootcamp:** low-ticket, practical introduction; great for testing the waters.
- **Accelerator:** the complete transformation — 14 days live, mentor-guided, ends with you
  shipping a real AI MVP, plus 12 months of weekly updates, community, buildathon, and the
  NSDC certificate (India). It wins on depth, projects, and support.
