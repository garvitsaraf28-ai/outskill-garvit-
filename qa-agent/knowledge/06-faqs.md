# Frequently asked questions

## "I'm not technical / I can't code — can I really do this?"

Yes. The AI Generalist Accelerator is built no-code, specifically for non-technical
professionals. Most learners start from zero technical background. The bar: **if you can use
Google Sheets, you can complete it.** Everything is taught live, step by step, with
recordings and office hours for anything you miss.

## "Am I too old for this? Will I keep up with the batch?"

About **76% of Generalist Accelerator learners have 10+ years of work experience** — the
cohort is experienced professionals, not fresh graduates. Retired executives and career-break
learners regularly complete it. Recordings + office hours mean nobody is left behind by pace.

## "I don't have time — my job is 50–60 hours a week."

Sessions are weekday evenings (7:30–11pm IST) and weekends, every session is recorded, and
there are two cohort start slots plus one free deferral. Learners with demanding jobs
typically watch recordings for sessions they miss and use office hours (Days 8–13) to catch
up. It's 14 days of focused effort, not a semester.

## "Can't I just learn this free on YouTube?"

You can learn *about* AI on YouTube. The Accelerator's difference is you **build**: a
structured 14-day path, live mentors who unblock you, a cohort, and a shipped AI MVP by Day 7
plus a 48-hour buildathon. Free content gives information; the program is designed to produce
a working portfolio and the habit of building — and then keeps you current with weekly live
update sessions for a year, because AI changes every week.

## "Is it live or recorded? What if I miss a session?"

Live, with a **recording of every session** available afterwards.

## "Is there a certificate?"

India: yes — NSDC / Skill India certified ("Artificial Intelligence Applications For Business
Leaders" for the Generalist program). **NSDC applies to India only**; international learners
should weigh the program on the portfolio, projects, mentors and community.

## "What exactly do I walk away with?"

A shipped AI MVP, buildathon experience, working automations and agents you built yourself,
12 months of the AI Content Library and weekly live AI-update sessions, the Kairos Business
Fellowship and Monetization Mastery 101, bonus tools (~$2,528 value), community access, and
(India) the NSDC certificate.

## "What's the refund policy?"

Full refund within 7 days of enrollment; after that you can defer to a later batch. For a
specific refund case or payment dispute, the agent connects you to the human team.

## "Will this get me a job / what salary can I expect?"

Nobody honest can guarantee a job or a specific salary, and Outskill doesn't. What can be
said: learners use the program to become the AI-capable person on their team, build side
income through freelancing/consulting, and ship a portfolio that demonstrates real skills.
Outcomes depend on your effort and market.

## "Which program should I pick — Generalist or Engineering?"

Can you comfortably write Python? → Engineering. Otherwise → Generalist (no-code). If you're
in between ("some scripting"), talk to the team and they'll route you based on what you want
to build.

## "When does the next batch start?"

Batch dates change per cohort. The team confirms current dates — ask in this chat and you'll
get the standing schedule pattern (weekday evenings + weekends) plus a pointer to confirm the
exact next start date.

## "Do you offer discounts or scholarships?"

No. There are no discounts and no scholarships. What does exist: zero-cost EMI in India
(3/6/9/12 months, Outskill bears the interest) and installments internationally (+9% fee),
so the fee can be spread out.

## "I paid / my payment failed / I want a refund — who do I talk to?"

Payment issues, refunds, and account-specific matters are handled by the human team — the
agent will point you to support rather than guess at your account state.
